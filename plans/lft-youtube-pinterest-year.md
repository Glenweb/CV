# LFT — YouTube & Pinterest, the 12-month plan

Month 1 = Oct 2026. Month 12 = Sep 2027. Written after `lft-search-diagnosis.md`, which
inverts the premise: these are not diversification any more, they are the traffic business.

## Four things that changed

1. **Search has returned its verdict.** ~28,000 impressions is a 12-month total, 17 clicks,
   85% of it one fan-out burst that died on 25 March. Real human queries rank 28–87.
2. **The embed map had to be re-derived.** The old one picked targets by impression pools
   that were machine queries. Embedding video where AI fan-out used to land buys nothing.
3. **The EU rule is adopted law, not a proposal.** Parliament 7 Jul 2026 (646–12–3), Council
   13 Jul 2026. Free personal item 40×30×15 under the seat, cabin bag to 100cm linear / 7kg,
   and airlines must show hand-baggage-inclusive fares at the start of booking. Application
   expected **23 Oct 2027** — one month past the end of this plan. **The whole year is the
   run-up to a dated, uncontested event. That is the calendar's spine.** Verify the Official
   Journal date and the application date at source before either goes on camera.
4. **The exclusivity claim is unverified.** Momondo has a web carry-on checker, not only an
   in-app AR tool. Fare-awareness may still be unique; "the only fare-aware checker" should
   not appear in a script until someone has opened Momondo's page.

### YouTube search

**Titles:** front-load the exact query, no cleverness. `easyJet Cabin Bag Size 2027 — What
Fits Free` beats `I Tested easyJet's Bag Policy`. Under 60 characters of useful text.
Shorts titles are the hook, not the keyword — different surface, different job.

**Thumbnails:** put the number in the image. `40×30×15`, `£75`, `2cm TOO BIG`. Red FAIL /
green PASS panel from the real checker UI. Legible at 120px. Three elements maximum.

**First 30 seconds**, every time: 0–3s the verdict not the setup (*"Your Ryanair bag is two
centimetres too big"*); 3–10s the stake (*"that's £75 at the gate"*); 10–20s the checker
screen with the real number — the product is the b-roll; 20–30s the promise and first
chapter. **No intro, no "hey guys", no channel animation. Ever.**

**End screens:** 20 seconds, two elements only — link to `/carry-on-size-checker/` with a
UTM, and the next video in the playlist. **No subscribe button**; it competes with the only
click that matters. **Pin a comment with the checker link on every video** — highest-CTR
link placement on YouTube, and it survives collapsed descriptions. Pitch the email list
*in-video* at the moment you say the fee number, not on the end screen; pitching both halves
both. Chapters written as questions, because chapter text is what gets indexed as a key moment.

## 3. Pinterest

### The blocker, first

**Zero of 122 published posts and pages has a featured image** (verified against the live
WordPress API, 28 Aug 2026). `featured_media` is empty on all 122, so `og:image` is absent
sitewide. **Rich Pins cannot work**, every share renders as a bare text link, and
Save-to-Pinterest has nothing to grab. Roughly two hours of work is gating an entire channel
for twelve months.

Week 1, in order:
1. Create **15 featured images at 1200×630** (the OG ratio — *not* the 1000×1500 pin ratio;
   you need both) for the 15 priority pages, set as WordPress Featured Image.
2. `curl` one page and grep for `property="og:image"`. If it's absent, RankMath's social
   settings aren't emitting it — fix that before making the other 14.
3. Confirm the Pinterest account is **Business** and the domain claim is **active**.
4. Run `developers.pinterest.com/tools/url-debugger` once. Rich Pins apply retroactively
   within ~24 hours.
5. Roll featured images to all 122 over months 1–3, ten a week, as background work.

This is a content edit, not a publish, so the 28 Aug publishing freeze doesn't block it.

## 4. Production — 3.5 hours a week

| Slot | Time | Work |
|---|---|---|
| Mon | 20 min | Read the three checker metrics. Nothing else. |
| Tue | 1.5 h | **Pinterest batch** — 10–15 fresh pins scheduled for the week |
| Thu | 1 h | YouTube: publish, descriptions, chapters, pinned comment, embed + schema |
| Sat | 40 min | Cut Shorts from the month's long-form |
| One day/month | 5 h | **Batch day** — all 5 Shorts and both long-form in one session |

One recording session feeds nine assets: 1 long-form → 3 Shorts → 1 video pin → 3 static
pins → 1 Kit email → 1 on-page embed. Finished Shorts auto-distribute to TikTok, Reels and
X through SocialBee. **Those three get zero planning time** — free distribution, not channels.

**Cut in this order when the week goes wrong:** Shorts first (most replaceable, batchable
two months at a time) · Pinterest down to 5 pins, never zero (cadence continuity beats
volume) · embed and schema slip a week. **Never cut** the monthly long-form, the Tuesday
batch existing at all, or the Monday data read. A bad month is 1 long-form, 0 Shorts, 20
pins — and **don't catch up the next month**; bursts suppress Pinterest distribution.

## 5. Review gates

Nothing here is measurable until the checker instrumentation ships. That is day one.

**Month 3 (31 Dec 26) — "is it measurable?"** The bar is instrumentation, not traffic.
Events firing in GA4; `og:image` on 15 pages with Rich Pins validated; channel rebranded
with 4 playlists, 6 long + 15 Shorts; 8 boards and ~120 pins with GA4 Pinterest sessions > 0;
≥1 video majority-viewed from YouTube search; ≥1 opt-in each from `source:pinterest` and
`source:youtube`.

*Kill criteria:* no video above 5% of views from YouTube search after 90 days → the titles
are wrong, **stop producing and rewrite every title and thumbnail before video 7**. Pinterest
referral still zero at week 12 → the URLs or Rich Pins are broken, debug the pipeline rather
than redesigning pins. **Do not kill either channel at month 3** — three months is one data point.

**Month 6 (31 Mar 27) — "which one, and what format?"** Metrics: GA4 sessions by
source/medium; `checker_result` and completion rate; **monetised action rate**
`(affiliate_click + email_optin) ÷ result`; Kit subscribers by source tag; branded-search
impressions for "luggage for travel"; and **opt-ins per production hour per channel**, which
is the allocation metric. Whichever channel wins that takes +50% of discretionary time from
month 7. Any format under 10% of channel views across six months stops — most likely
talking-head long-form and Idea Pins.

**Month 12 (30 Sep 27) — "is this the business?"** **Non-Google sessions as a share of
total** is the number that decides whether the year worked; target majority. Also: list size
and opt-in split, monetised action rate trended, branded search month 1 vs 12, and
**referring domains — currently zero**. If the checker has earned even a handful through its
embed generator, that is the authority ceiling moving for the first time and the most
valuable outcome of the year.

*Kill at month 12:* if neither channel shows sustained month-on-month growth in sessions and
opt-ins across months 6–12, the problem is the offer, not the channels — and the next
question is product, not distribution.
