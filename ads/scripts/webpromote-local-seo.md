## Hook variants

1. **Proximity (default):** "Someone in your town just searched for what you do."
2. **Loss:** "Your competitor is three doors down and two places above you on Google."
3. **Offer-led:** "Send me your business name and town. I'll tell you why you're not in the top three."

Variant 3 is the direct-response play — lower reach, much higher lead rate. For cold LinkedIn and Meta, open with 1. For retargeting, switch to 3.

## Targeting

- **LinkedIn:** job titles Owner / Director / Founder; company size 1–50; UK; industries construction, consumer services, facilities, automotive repair.
- **Meta:** UK, 28–60, interests in small-business ownership and trade bodies. Advantage+ with the detailed targeting as a suggestion, not a cage.
- **Google Search:** this creative is for Demand Gen and YouTube. Search needs text ads against `[local seo agency uk]`, `[google business profile management]` and competitor-brand terms.
