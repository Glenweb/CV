## Claims audit — read this before you launch

| Claim | Status |
|---|---|
| "Both are free to join" | ✅ True of both free tiers. Quidco has a paid premium tier — the creative says "free, no premium tier" only of TopCashback, which is correct. |
| "More retailers" (TCB) | ⚠️ Directionally true on published counts — **verify both numbers on the day you launch** and keep the claim comparative, never numeric. |
| "Faster payouts" (Quidco) | ⚠️ Same — comparative only, no day counts on screen. |
| "Bigger welcome bonus" | ⚠️ Promotional and volatile. **Pull this line if the bonuses level up.** |
| No earnings figures anywhere | ✅ Deliberate. "Earn £300 a year" is an ASA problem and a Meta financial-claims problem. Don't add it. |
