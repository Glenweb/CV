Spirit Airlines stopped flying on 2 May 2026. There is no current Spirit carry-on size, because there are no Spirit flights. If you are here because you bought a bag to fit Spirit's limits, or you had a booking, both are answered below.

The short answer

  • Spirit's free personal item was 45 × 35 × 20 cm (about 18 × 14 × 8 in) — one of the stricter free allowances in the US market.
  • Spirit's paid carry-on was 56 × 46 × 25 cm (about 22 × 18 × 10 in).
  • Both are now historical. No airline is honouring Spirit tickets or Spirit bag fees.
  • If you own a bag sized for Spirit, it fits as a free personal item on Frontier and as an included carry-on on Southwest, Delta, American, Alaska and JetBlue (on JetBlue, Blue fares and above). Details in the table below.

What happened to Spirit

Spirit ceased all operations on 2 May 2026, ending a 34-year run. The airline had filed for Chapter 11 bankruptcy protection twice — in November 2024 and again in August 2025 — and a federal support package worth roughly $500 million did not come through. Rising jet fuel costs were cited among the causes. More than 17,000 jobs went with it.

Spirit was the airline that made the unbundled fare normal in the United States: a cheap seat, and a separate charge for the bag in the overhead bin. That model outlived the airline — Frontier and Allegiant still run it, and the US majors now sell Basic Economy fares that work the same way.

If you had a Spirit booking

This is what was reported at the time of the shutdown. Confirm your own case with your card issuer — do not rely on a summary.

  • Booked directly with Spirit by credit or debit card: Spirit said it would process refunds automatically, and that most were completed within days of the shutdown announcement.
  • Booked through a travel agency, OTA or third party: the refund goes back through whoever took the payment. Chase that company, not the airline.
  • No refund arriving: a chargeback through your card issuer is the route. In the US, the Fair Credit Billing Act covers goods and services not delivered. UK cardholders should ask their issuer about chargeback or Section 75.
  • Vouchers, travel credits and Free Spirit points: these are unsecured claims and go through the bankruptcy process. Realistically, do not plan around recovering them.

Who flies Spirit's old routes now

The network did not disappear — it was absorbed, mostly by the other low-cost carriers.

  • JetBlue took the largest share, picking up nine former Spirit routes and expanding substantially at Fort Lauderdale (FLL), including FLL to Houston Intercontinental, Nashville, Baltimore, Charlotte, Orlando and Chicago O'Hare.
  • Breeze Airways took most of Spirit's Atlantic City routes.
  • Frontier, United, Delta and Southwest all announced expansions into Spirit markets.

Practically: if you used to fly Spirit out of Fort Lauderdale, look at JetBlue first. If you flew out of Atlantic City, look at Breeze. If you were flying Spirit purely on price, Frontier is the closest like-for-like — same unbundled model, same gate-side bag sizers.

Read the two rows that matter

  • Frontier's free personal item is 46 × 36 × 20 cm — a centimetre wider and taller than Spirit's. If your bag passed on Spirit, it passes on Frontier. The enforcement is at least as aggressive, so a bag that only just passed on Spirit will only just pass here.
  • United Basic Economy is the trap. At 43 × 25 × 23 cm, the free box is much narrower than Spirit's. A soft 45 × 35 × 20 cm backpack that lived under a Spirit seat will not pass as a United Basic Economy personal item.
  • Southwest is the only carrier here that includes a full-size carry-on in every fare, at the largest dimensions in the table. If you are replacing Spirit on price-plus-bag rather than headline fare, price Southwest before Frontier.

Frequently asked questions

What was Spirit's carry-on size?

Spirit's free personal item was 45 × 35 × 20 cm (about 18 × 14 × 8 in) and had to fit under the seat. A larger carry-on for the overhead bin, 56 × 46 × 25 cm (about 22 × 18 × 10 in), had to be paid for. Spirit ceased operations on 2 May 2026, so these limits no longer apply to any flight.

Is Spirit Airlines still flying?

No. Spirit ceased all operations on 2 May 2026 after two Chapter 11 bankruptcy filings. It is not flying under its own name and no other airline is operating Spirit flights.

Can I get a refund on a Spirit ticket?

Bookings made directly with Spirit by credit or debit card were refunded automatically after the shutdown. Bookings made through a travel agency or online travel site are refunded by that company. If no refund arrives, a chargeback through your card issuer is the usual route. Vouchers, travel credits and Free Spirit points are handled through the bankruptcy process and are unlikely to be recovered.

Which airline is most like Spirit now?

Frontier Airlines. It runs the same unbundled model — a cheap base fare with the cabin bag charged separately — and its free personal item, at 46 × 36 × 20 cm, is marginally larger than Spirit's was. Allegiant Air is similar but allows a smaller free personal item at 41 × 38 × 18 cm.

Does my Spirit-sized bag still work?

On most airlines, yes, and often with room to spare. A bag built for Spirit's 45 × 35 × 20 cm personal item fits Frontier's free allowance and fits comfortably inside the included carry-on size on Southwest, Delta, American, Alaska and JetBlue's Blue fares. The exception is United Basic Economy, whose free personal item is only 43 × 25 × 23 cm.
