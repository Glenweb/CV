The free bag is the whole game

Frontier's personal item at 46 × 36 × 20 cm is larger than most US carriers allow for free, and it is the closest match to what Spirit used to permit. A well-chosen under-seat bag at this size means never buying a Frontier carry-on.

  • Structured, not soft. Frontier is in the strictest enforcement band and sizes personal items at boarding.
  • A centimetre under on every axis. 46 × 36 × 20 cm advertised is usually over once packed.
  • Front-loading over top-loading — you will be retrieving things from a bag wedged under a seat.

If you are buying the paid carry-on anyway

Frontier's paid allowance is 61 × 41 × 25 cm with a 15.9 kg limit — generous on size, and unusually one of the few US carriers to publish a cabin weight limit at all. Pick a lightweight shell: a 3 kg bag leaves 12.9 kg of packing against 11.4 kg for a 4.5 kg one.

Frequently asked questions

What is Frontier's free personal item size?

46 × 36 × 20 cm (18 × 14 × 8 in), free on every fare, and it must fit under the seat.

How big is Frontier's paid carry-on?

61 × 41 × 25 cm (24 × 16 × 10 in) with a 15.9 kg weight limit. It is cheapest booked with the flight and most expensive at the gate.

Does Frontier check personal item size?

Yes. Frontier is in the strictest enforcement band in our index and sizes personal items at boarding.
