Lufthansa's cabin allowance depends entirely on your fare. Economy Light gets a hand bag and nothing else — that is the detail that catches people out.

What you get, by fare

FareCabin allowance
Economy Light Hand bag only: 40 × 30 × 15 cm (≈16 × 12 × 6 in), 8 kg
Economy Classic and above Cabin bag 55 × 40 × 23 cm (≈22 × 16 × 9 in), 8 kg, plus a 40 × 30 × 15 cm hand bag

Both figures are external and include wheels and handles.

The Economy Light trap

Economy Light is sold as the cheap fare, and on a search page it looks like the others. It is not: there is no cabin bag, only the 40 × 30 × 15 cm hand bag that goes under the seat. Turning up with a 55 × 40 × 23 cm roller on an Economy Light ticket means paying at the airport.

Before you book Light, price Classic with the bag you intend to bring. The gap is often smaller than the airport charge.

Enforcement: conditional

Lufthansa sits in our visual-check band, not the aggressive one. Bags are screened by eye as you board and pulled to the sizer only if they look clearly oversized. A bag comfortably inside 55 × 40 × 23 cm usually walks on unchallenged — which is precisely why the 8 kg weight limit, not the dimensions, is the one that catches people on full long-haul flights.

Frequently asked questions

What is Lufthansa's carry-on size?

55 × 40 × 23 cm (22 × 16 × 9 in) with a 8 kg limit, on Economy Classic and above. You may also bring a 40 × 30 × 15 cm hand bag.

Can I bring a cabin bag on Lufthansa Economy Light?

No. Economy Light includes a 40 × 30 × 15 cm hand bag that must fit under the seat. A full cabin bag requires Economy Classic or higher, or paying for it separately.

How strict is Lufthansa about cabin baggage?

Moderately. Bags are visually screened at boarding and sized only if they look oversized. The 8 kg weight limit is enforced more consistently than the dimensions.
