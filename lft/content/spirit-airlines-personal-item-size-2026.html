Spirit Airlines ceased operations on 2 May 2026. Its personal item limit was 45 × 35 × 20 cm (about 18 × 14 × 8 in). That number no longer applies to any flight you can book. The three things worth knowing

1. Allegiant is the exact swap, Frontier the near one

Allegiant's free personal item is 45 × 35 × 20 cm — precisely Spirit's number, on every axis. A bag built to pass a Spirit sizer passes Allegiant's, and misses it by the same margin if it was borderline.

Frontier's is 46 × 36 × 20 cm — a centimetre larger than Spirit's in two dimensions and identical in the third, so it also passes. Both enforce at least as hard as Spirit did, so a bag that only just squeezed in before will only just squeeze in now — expect the sizer, and expect it to be used.

2. United Basic Economy is where Spirit refugees get caught

The free box is 43 × 25 × 23 cm. The 25 cm is the problem: Spirit's allowance was 35 cm across, so a standard under-seat backpack or 18 × 14 × 8 in holdall is 10 cm too wide. If you are shopping on price and United Basic Economy comes up cheapest, add the carry-on fee before you compare — or price a standard United fare, which includes the full 56 × 35 × 23 cm carry-on.

3. Southwest changes the maths entirely

Southwest includes a full 61 × 41 × 25 cm carry-on in every fare — the largest cabin allowance of any airline in this comparison, free. Against Spirit's model (cheap seat plus a paid bag), a Southwest fare that looks more expensive on the search page is frequently cheaper once the bag is in the basket. If you only ever flew Spirit because of the headline price, this is the comparison to run.

Spirit's limits, for the record

  • Free personal item: 45 × 35 × 20 cm (≈18 × 14 × 8 in), under the seat.
  • Paid carry-on: 56 × 46 × 25 cm (≈22 × 18 × 10 in), overhead bin.
  • Enforcement: among the strictest in the US market — gate sizers used routinely, and gate-side fees well above the online price.

Frequently asked questions

What was Spirit's personal item size?

45 × 35 × 20 cm, or about 18 × 14 × 8 in, and it had to fit under the seat in front of you. It was included in every Spirit fare. Spirit ceased operations on 2 May 2026, so the limit no longer applies to any bookable flight.

Which airline has the same personal item size as Spirit?

Frontier Airlines is the closest, at 46 × 36 × 20 cm — marginally larger than Spirit's in two dimensions and the same depth. American Airlines Basic Economy uses the same 46 × 36 × 20 cm box.

Will my Spirit personal item bag fit on other airlines?

In most cases, comfortably. It fits Frontier's and American Basic Economy's free personal item, and fits within the included carry-on allowance on Southwest, Delta, Alaska, American, United and JetBlue standard fares (United standard is 35 cm wide, exactly matching Spirit's 35 cm, so leave the straps loose). It is too wide for United Basic Economy, whose free personal item is 43 × 25 × 23 cm. On Allegiant it is an exact fit, at 45 × 35 × 20 cm.

Which airline gives the biggest free cabin bag?

Southwest Airlines, at 61 × 41 × 25 cm (about 24 × 16 × 10 in), included in every fare with no separate carry-on charge.

Is Spirit Airlines flying in 2026?

Spirit flew until 2 May 2026 and then ceased all operations. It is not operating now and no other airline is flying under the Spirit name.
