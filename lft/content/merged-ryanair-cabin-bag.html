Ryanair has two cabin allowances and they need two different bags. This page covers both, because buying the wrong one is how people end up paying at the gate.

Bags for the free under-seat allowance (40 × 30 × 20 cm)

This is the one most people need, and the one most people get wrong. You are shopping for a bag whose packed external size stays inside 40 × 30 × 20 cm.

What matters, in order:

  1. A structured shape, not a squashy one. A bag that holds its form is measurable and predictable at the gate. A soft holdall expands exactly where the sizer is tightest.
  2. Nominal size at or just under the limit. A bag advertised at 40 × 30 × 20 cm exactly will usually measure over once packed. Aim a centimetre under.
  3. No external pockets that bulge. They are part of the measurement.
  4. Low empty weight. There is no published weight limit on this allowance, but a heavy empty bag wastes the capacity you are paying for.

Bags for the Priority cabin allowance (55 × 40 × 20 cm)

A 10 kg limit applies here, so empty weight matters as much as dimensions. A 2.5 kg shell leaves 7.5 kg of packing; a 4 kg one leaves 6 kg. That difference is a pair of shoes and a coat.

  • Two-wheel over four-wheel for this size — spinner castors add depth and eat into the 20 cm axis.
  • Soft or hybrid over hard shell, because hard shells are heavier for the same capacity and you are weight-limited.
  • No expansion zip, or at least never use it. An expanded bag is an oversized bag.

Buying for both at once

If you fly Ryanair on both fare types, the efficient combination is one structured under-seat bag at 40 × 30 × 20 cm and one lightweight 55 × 40 × 20 cm cabin bag — not a single mid-sized bag that is too big for the free allowance and wasteful of the paid one.

Frequently asked questions

What size bag is free on Ryanair?

40 × 30 × 20 cm (16 × 12 × 8 in), under the seat, on every fare.

Do I need Priority to bring a cabin bag on Ryanair?

To bring a 55 × 40 × 20 cm bag into the overhead locker, yes — Priority & 2 Cabin Bags or a paid cabin-bag add-on. Without it you get the 40 × 30 × 20 cm under-seat bag only.

What is the weight limit for a Ryanair cabin bag?

10 kg for the Priority cabin bag. Ryanair publishes no weight limit for the free under-seat bag.
